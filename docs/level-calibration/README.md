# Output and effect-level calibration

Local 1.0.2-dev, baseline 9ec50f7, FM-1 build C1F004AF. Build IDs do not include every DSP source input: use the exact SHA-256 pins in levels-artifacts.json. Public 1.0.1 is unchanged.

## What changed

The raw synth already occupied almost the entire digital range at maximum polyphony. In the original WASM build, algorithm 12, four operators at level 127, velocity 127, C4 and feedback 0/127 measured about -13.21 dBFS for one voice and -0.12 dBFS for seven aligned voices. A global boost would sacrifice polyphonic headroom. The oscillator/envelope/routing renderer and its existing summed-voice soft knee are unchanged; a final 0.90 scale reserves output headroom.

A stereo-linked, one-block lookahead peak stage follows the effects, performance controls and metronome, before MASTER and integer conversion. Its ceiling is 0.8912509 (-1 dBFS sample peak). It delays both channels by 128 samples (2.90249 ms). The previous block's safe gain is known before playback; the gain ramp endpoint is constrained by both previous and arriving block requirements. Normal signals pass with their waveform and dynamics intact, apart from fixed scaling/delay. Exceptional peaks reduce gain with a linear anticipatory ramp and approximately 200 ms release. NaN/Inf are replaced by zero. Panic and flash-maintenance entry clear the delay. This replaces dependence on the old final hard clamp; that clamp remains as a defensive integer-conversion guard. MASTER and fixed-level USB recording retain their existing relationship.

Effects use smoothed, post-slot makeup to keep output near the input level. Distortion uses parameter/input-power calibration; chorus, flanger, phaser, delay and reverb use interpolated parameter calibration. Gain bounds are approximately 0.25..4.0. Input-power knots were measured at four operator levels (64,85,105,127); knob grid points are 0,32,64,96,127. Parameter interpolation is cached until knobs change. No gain is placed ahead of the first effect, preserving its source drive. Correcting slot 1 also changes the signal level presented to slot 2, as intended for a level-matched serial chain.

Wah needed an additional treatment: independent high-resonance settings escaped the coarse grid by about 9 dB. It now uses approximately 56 ms input/output power tracking, bounded inverse-square-root gain calculation and approximately 11 ms gain smoothing. Makeup holds during silence/tails. The binary32 seed/two-Newton-step helper is validated against sqrt at 100001 ratios over [1/16,16], with relative error below 1e-5. It adds no libm or double call. The target uses two bounded four-byte memcpy calls for defined float-bit conversion. The old special -12.04 dB MoDemLead trim is removed, including its application to saved legacy trim flags; the flags/schema remain readable. In the final C4/velocity-100 audit MoDemLead dry/wet RMS differs by about 0.08 dB.

## Measurements and limits

Data are actual application WebAssembly/worklet output at 44.1 kHz, MASTER full, stereo RMS and sample peak. RMS is a level proxy, not LUFS or a promise of equal perceived loudness across different spectra/presets. The training chord is C/G/C/E at velocity 127; factory audit includes other notes, velocity and seven-note chords. Tables are bounded approximations, not exhaustive equalization of every patch. Phaser Lab's extra diagnostic tuning was not fitted. No hardware analog/USB capture or oversampled true-peak measurement was made.

- Frozen original measurements: raw-baseline.json, fx-baseline.json and fx-extra-baseline.json. Baseline app identity is in ../hint-case-artifacts.json. There are 454 FX requests; ten delay/time-127 requests exceed the existing arena and were fallback measurements. They are explicitly marked and excluded from fitting. The unreachable far table column extends the last valid time measurement; no delay allocation limits were changed.
- Independent final validation: 24 maximum-output cases (feedback 0/127, both types, notes 36/60/84, one/seven voices), 156 intermediate knob/input-level cases, and all 64 two-slot combinations at their tested settings, including tails. All 64 chains were accepted; expanded native chain tests also use 4.0-amplitude stress input and arena canaries.
- Factory/Init audit: 528 dry/wet cases, velocities 100/127, notes 48/60/72 and a seven-note chord.
- All 772 final emulator cases remain under the -1 dBFS sample ceiling; maximum observed absolute sample 0.89124668. Independent effect-vs-dry RMS deltas span **-1.36..+1.88 dB**. Wah spans about -0.03..+0.14 dB on those cases. These ranges describe the tested source/settings, not all possible incoming sounds.

Per-effect and exact case results: summary.json, validation.json and presets.json. Scripts: tools/measure-levels.mjs and measure-extra-levels.mjs collect reference output; derive-level-calibration.mjs regenerates the table from frozen original data (do not refit against already-calibrated output). test-levels-emu.mjs and audit-preset-levels.mjs validate the current build; summarize-level-calibration.mjs makes the report. The original probe did not request readback after rejected delay settings; final tools read back the actual common patch and skip or reject mismatched settings.
