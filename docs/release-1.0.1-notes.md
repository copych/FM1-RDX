# FM1-RDX 1.0.1

Save sounds and musical ideas directly on your FM-1, with a consistent Reface-style pixel display.

- 32 named user voice slots, separate from the factory sounds.
- Phrase recording, playback and overdubbing with a captured voice for the whole loop and independent live preset selection; effects remain shared.
- Four persistent musical draft slots storing the phrase, voice and tempo.
- Audible metronome with OFF, AUTO and ON modes.
- LEDs for notes, recording, beats, menus and draft availability.
- Unified monochrome bitmap GUI for editing, Store, Looper and Drafts.
- Octave shifts preserve held notes; PRESET works directly in the looper.
- Up to seven adaptive voices, USB MIDI, Soundmondo and stereo 44.1 kHz/16-bit USB recording.
- Matching browser emulator with persistent voice and draft storage.

Press SAVE to store a voice, REC to record, SEQ to open the looper and ARP to save or recall a draft. Stop playback before saving; flash writes briefly mute sound. Close the installer tab before opening a MIDI or recording app.

[Play in your browser](https://copych.github.io/FM1-RDX/emu/) · [Install on FM-1](https://copych.github.io/FM1-RDX/install/)

The firmware is tested regularly on FM-1 hardware. The new pixel UI still needs its own physical readability and seven-voice UI stress check. Build and regression details are in docs/release-1.0.1.md.
