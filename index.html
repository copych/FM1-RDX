Switch on. Get curious.

Experimental preview

The browser synth is ready to explore. An initial hardware test passed; the latest fixes await a hardware retest. Patch saving, USB audio and pitch bend/mod wheel input are not available yet. Back up your current instrument before installing. The installer also explains how to return to stock firmware.

Made by copych, based on RDX for ESP32, fm1-base and felucca-bsp. Independent community firmware, not an official Yamaha or M-VAVE product.
